Reconnaissance — passive + active, scoped to your allow-list

47 data sources fan out in parallel. Subdomain enumeration, certificate transparency, ASN mapping, exposed-service detection. Everything discovered is tagged with confidence and provenance so downstream decisions are auditable.

$ bulwark recon --target acme.com --profile stealth [14:02:11] 47 sources initialized [14:02:14] subdomain enum · cert-transparency · asn-mapping [14:03:48] discovered 23 hosts · 11 services · 3 in-scope [14:03:48] asset inventory committed to audit-log

Vulnerability match — stack fingerprint, not generic CVEs

Every host is fingerprinted against your actual stack before templates fire. A Solr box gets Solr-specific checks, not a generic Apache CVE. Templates refresh daily from NVD, GHSA, nuclei, and vendor advisories.

$ bulwark match --inventory inv-a3f9c1.json [14:08:22] fingerprint → apache-solr 8.11.2 [14:08:24] loaded 14,302 templates · vendor-aware filter [14:08:31] matched 7 candidates · 2 exploit-reachable [14:08:31] candidates committed to audit-log

Verification — every finding reproducibly confirmed

No "potentially vulnerable" hedging. Each candidate gets a controlled verification run: payload logged, request/response captured, artifact hashed, timestamp preserved. Failed verification gets dropped, not papered over.

$ bulwark verify --candidate CAND-014 [14:11:45] payload: cve-2025-24813-rce.sh [14:11:47] capturing response · sha256:9f2a...c4b1 [14:11:47] ✓ verified · evidence sealed · finding drafted [14:11:48] audit-log entry appended

How BULWARK runs.

Single binary, four primitives. The runner is a sandboxed process on a hardened image; the audit log is append-only with hash-chained entries; the report builder is offline and reproducible.

Questions we hear most.

If yours isn't here, ping us at hello@getwark.com — we usually reply within a day.

Is this a SaaS or on-prem?
Both. Starter and Continuous run on our hosted runner (loopback-only, egress-pinned). Enterprise gets a binary that runs in your VPC or air-gapped — same code, no telemetry.
+
Do you do internal pentests?
No — BULWARK is external-only. For internal assessments, we partner with two firms we trust; ask us for an intro.
+
What if BULWARK finds nothing?
That's a real outcome, and we report it as such — no "we found N informational findings" padding. You get a signed attestation instead, useful for compliance.
+
How do you handle out-of-scope hits?
Logged, skipped, never exploited. Every out-of-scope detection is hashed and added to the audit log so you can verify the agent saw it and moved on.
+
Can I see a sample report?
Yes — sanitized redacted samples are in the docs. For a tailored sample scoped to your stack, ask in the scoping call.
+
What's your false positive rate?
Zero false positives in delivered reports since v3 (Q1 2024). Every finding is reproducibly verified before it ships. Candidates that don't verify are dropped from the report entirely.
+

Run a pentest without staffing one.

Continuous external coverage from $28k/quarter. Or scope a one-shot sweep from $4.9k. Either way, you get a signed report your auditors can verify and your engineers can act on.
