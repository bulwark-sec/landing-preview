Vulnerability disclosure.

We commit to working with security researchers who report vulnerabilities to us in good faith. Below is the policy, scope, and the timeline we hold ourselves to.

Scope

  • *.getwark.com — main marketing site and dashboard
  • *.bulwark-sec.github.io/landing-preview — this preview site
  • The BULWARK CLI binary distributed from getwark.com
  • The BULWARK webapp at app.getwark.com

Out of scope

  • Social engineering of BULWARK employees or customers
  • Physical attacks against BULWARK infrastructure
  • DDoS, volumetric, or resource-exhaustion attacks
  • Third-party integrations (report to the vendor directly)
  • Vulnerabilities in dependencies with no exploitable path to BULWARK

How to report

Send your report to security@getwark.com with PGP key 0x4B2E 91F3 8A7D 6C54.

Our commitments

  • 24 hours — acknowledgment of your report
  • 72 hours — initial triage and severity classification
  • 14 days — fix or mitigation plan for critical and high severity
  • 90 days — public disclosure deadline (or earlier with your consent)

Safe harbor

Research conducted under this policy is considered authorized. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who comply with this policy in good faith.
